# Plain summaries

Use this when the user asks for a summary, a TL;DR or "the gist" of a text.

## Steps

1. Read the whole text once before writing anything.
2. Write a one-line headline that says what the text is about and why it matters.
3. Write at most five points. One idea per point, short words, no jargon.
4. If the text asks the reader to do something, end with a line that starts "To do:".
5. Do not add facts that are not in the text.

## Example

Headline: The council will close Elm Road for repairs in March.

- Work starts on 3 March and lasts two weeks.
- Buses use Oak Street instead.
- Homes on Elm Road keep car access after 6 pm.

To do: move cars parked on Elm Road before 3 March.
