## Proposal S14 (owner idea, 2026-11-15): long music video on a freestyle board

A 4 minute music video made in one go with a generated song, planned on a loose board: pick any picture, chat with a model about that picture to tone it up, and keep one overall chat for direction.

Parts that already exist: Music (ACE-Step song from lyrics and style), Storyboard (shots, takes, passes from S6), the Mixer (audio on a timeline), Voice, LTX clips, Chat with Studio tools.
Parts that are new:
1. A music-video project type: song first, then the length and a cut plan from the song (sections and beats found from the audio), so shots are as long as the music asks.
2. A freestyle board view: pictures as cards on a canvas, free layout, then "order these" into the timeline. Per-card chat that edits that card's prompt or picture; one board chat for the whole direction.
3. Scale: 4 minutes at 4 s a clip is about 60 clips. On this Mac that is hours (Iris stills about 15 s each, LTX-2.5 clips about 2 min each, so roughly 2 to 3 hours plus the song), so it needs a queue that survives restarts (S3 helps), a pause and resume, and cheap drafts first (S6).
4. Lip-sync and character consistency across 60 shots are the quality risks; Cast and reference images help, lip-sync is not built.
Size: a feature of its own (est. 2 to 3 weeks), best as 0.11.0 with S6 and S8 to S11 as the base. Not part of 0.10.0.

## S15 Style reference and brand kit (approved 2026-11-15)

For an advert in the look of a client's website or brand: a third reference kind, `style`, next to character and place. The user uploads screenshots or pictures; the project keeps a small brand kit (palette read from the pictures, optional logo, optional caption look: font choice from the installed set, colour, position). Shots and the Plan step (S8) use the style references when they write prompts and when the image model accepts reference pictures; where the model cannot take references, the style is written into the prompt as words (palette and mood) and the shot says so. The Export tab can add the logo and a title or end card. No website scraping; pictures only (a screenshot), so nothing is fetched from the web.
