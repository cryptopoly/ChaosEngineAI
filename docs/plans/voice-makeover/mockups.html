Voice makeover: mock-ups

Proposed screens for Voice > Models, Studio (Speak, Voices, Transcribe) and Library, plus the Voice popover in Chat, for the state after W6. Dark theme, colours and type from src/styles.css. Click the tabs, the voice row, the row menus and the Change buttons. Nothing here is wired to the app. Companion: review.md (findings) and plan.md (work packages).

1. Voice > Models one page: runtime, dictation models, speech models, with Delete everywhere

Changes: the pack is named by what it is (a runtime, with the two small add-ons chosen at install); dictation language sits with the dictation models; memory sits with the runtime; the quick voices are a model row like any other; every row has a menu with Delete; the speech models are grouped by family so nine rows read as five choices.

Shown inside the frame above: click Voices in the Studio header.
