## 3. Open questions for the owner

1. **Put the speech models on the shared Models table (M3b, L) rather than a voice-only table (M3, M)?** Default: **yes, M3b.** It is the one place the app already solved search, filters, Fit and Delete; the voice-only table would be a second copy. The earlier decision to keep a custom page (`docs/plans/voice-rework/plan.md:44`) predates nine rows and four families.
2. **Where do the chat behaviour settings live: the Voice popover in Chat (S3) or stay on Voices?** Default: **Chat.** They change how Chat reads and calls; a person looks for them in Chat. Voices keeps only which voice does what.
3. **Show the add-ons as tick boxes at install time (M4) and let unticking remove them?** Default: **yes.** The backend already takes `extras`; removing an extra is a pip uninstall of its packages plus a marker edit.
4. **Offer Remove voice runtime in the app (M5)?** Default: **yes, under Troubleshooting, with a confirm** that names what stops working and keeps downloaded models by default.
5. **Rename "Voice pack" to "Voice runtime" and drop the product names from its subtitle?** Default: **yes.** "Pack" reads as "the models"; the dictation and speech models are separate downloads on the same page. Product names stay on the rows where they are models.
6. **Order the Studio modes Speak, Voices, Transcribe (Voices second)?** Default: **yes.** After W6 the Voices tab is where the main voices are chosen, which a newcomer does before anything else; Transcribe users keep the remembered mode.
