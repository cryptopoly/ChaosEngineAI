# Voice makeover: review (D-73)

Date: 2026-11-09. Branch `review/voice-makeover`, cut from `release/v0.10.0` (`c804f8e6`). Review and design only; no app code changed. Designed for the state after W6 (`feat/voice-w6`: any listed voice in Chat read-aloud and calls, a voice per use, Kokoro as the fallback, D-72).

Inputs: the code on the branch, the W6 branch diff (backend only so far: `routes/voice.py`, `voice_chat.py`, `voice_speak.py`, `voice_clone.py`, `voice_runtime.py`, the catalog), `docs/plans/voice-ux-round2.md`, `docs/plans/voice-models-wave2.md`, `docs/usage/voice.md`, and screenshots of every Voice screen taken with the Playwright harness against the shared voice mocks (`e2e/tests/voiceMocks.ts` plus the wave-2 rows, Vite 8951, backend 8952, throwaway HOME, 1280 px and 375 px). Screenshots are in [shots/](shots/) (`d-` desktop, `m-` phone). Mock-ups of the proposal: [mockups.html](mockups.html). Work packages: [plan.md](plan.md).

Line numbers are from this branch's working tree.

## 1. How a first-time user meets Voice today

The sidebar says **Voice: Models, Studio, Library** with the caption "STT, TTS, and voice chat" (`src/constants/sidebarGroups.ts:18`) and the Models page header "STT and TTS model management" (`src/constants/tabs.ts:46`). Two acronyms before the person has clicked anything.

1. **Models** (`d-models-no-pack.png`): one panel, "Voice pack: Apple Silicon: Parakeet and Whisper on MLX, Kokoro voices", a badge "Not installed", a sentence about "its own environment (about 2 GB, plus the models you download)" and **Install voice pack**. Three product names (Parakeet, Whisper, Kokoro) and one framework (MLX) are the only explanation of what the pack is. The person installs it because it is the only button.
2. **Models, installed** (`d-models-installed.png`, 2,200 px tall): five panels in a column: Voice pack; Speech-to-text models (five cards, each "Use for dictation" plus Download); Speech models (nine cards, each with a five-cell cost table, capability chips and one button); Quick voices (one line: "Kokoro: 49 voices in 8 languages, part of the voice pack, under 1 GB", Add Mandarin voices, Browse voices); Dictation and memory (dictation language, idle minutes, Unload now). Nothing on the page can delete anything.
3. **Studio** (`d-speak-natural.png`, 1,660 px): header "Voice Studio" twice (page title and shell title), modes Speak, Transcribe, Voices. Speak: the text box, then five picker groups (Recommended, Quick, Cloned, Designed, More: up to 30 cards), then the style box, chips, sliders, Advanced, the model line, Generate. At 1280x900 the style box and Generate are below the fold; at 375 px (`m-speak-natural.png`, 2,800 px) the style box is the last thing on the page.
4. **Voices** (`d-voices.png`, 2,360 px): four action cards; the cloning statement as a paragraph; filters All / Recommended / Quick / Natural / Cloned / Designed; a full-width language select; a note "Chat reads aloud with quick voices for now"; then 28 rows in list order (the 20 American quick voices first, Ryan and the other natural voices at row 21), each row with a sample button, a primary **Use in Speak** and a **Use in Chat**; the agent-cloning switch; and a "Chat and calls" settings panel at the bottom.
5. **Library** (`d-library-speech.png`): fine. Cards name the voice and its kind, have Speak again, Play, Export, Delete and Start a storyboard. The only gap is no voice-kind filter.
6. **Chat** (`d-chat.png`): mic button, Call button (greyed with no model) and an (i). Nothing says which voice will read, and the (i) text sends people to "Voice Models" for a voice (`src/features/chat/ChatTab.tsx:217-218`), where no voice can be chosen since W5 moved them to Voices.

## 2. Findings, ranked by how much they confuse a newcomer

Evidence in brackets. The first eight are the ones to fix.

1. **Nothing in Voice can be deleted except cloned voices and library items.** Speech models (up to 25 GB across nine rows), dictation models (up to 5 GB), the Mandarin add-on and the 2 GB pack have Download but no Delete (`src/features/voice/speak/TtsModelsPanel.tsx:70-92`, `VoiceModelsTab.tsx:108-122`, `VoicePackPanel.tsx:146-170`; no delete route in `backend_service/routes/voice.py` or `routes/voice_clone.py`; `voice_clone.py` has `delete_voice` only). Image, Video, Music and Chat Models all have **Delete model** in the row action menu with a shared confirm (`src/components/models/downloadActions.ts:50`, `deleteConfirm.ts`). A person who downloaded Orpheus to try it has no way back short of Finder. [`d-models-installed.png`]
2. **Speak buries its own controls under the picker.** The five groups sit between the text box and the style box; the model line and Generate are the last things on the page (`SpeakScreen.tsx:267-280` picker, `:282` style, `:401` model line, `:484` Generate). On a 1280x900 window the fold cuts through the Cloned group; at 375 px the style box is 2,500 px down. The picker is also the only control with no "current value" summary, so after choosing a voice there is nothing near Generate that says which voice will run except the small model line. Worse, the per-model controls are split around the picker: the sound-tag chips of Chatterbox-Turbo and Orpheus sit *above* it, under the text box (`SpeakScreen.tsx:265`), while the style box, the Expression slider, the Speech language select and the **Cloning model** select sit *below* it (`:282-399`). With Ada selected the cloning model is chosen at the bottom of the page (Chatterbox-Turbo, by the smallest-installed rule) and the tag chips it enables appear at the top, 1,300 px away; the greyed style box in between says "Cloned voices have no style control". [`d-speak-natural.png`, `m-speak-natural.png`, `d-speak-turbo.png`, `d-speak-clone.png`]
3. **The Voices tab is a wall of 28 identical rows with two buttons each, in the wrong order.** `managerVoices()` keeps list order (`speakLogic.ts:227-229`), so the 20 American quick voices (ungraded order: Alloy, Aoede, Jessica, Kore before Nicole) come first and the natural voices the app recommends are at row 21. Every row carries a primary "Use in Speak" (28 primary buttons on one screen) and "Use in Chat"; after W6 a third use (Calls) is coming (`callVoice`, W6 `routes/voice.py:69-74`), which would make it three buttons per row. The shortlist logic from round two exists (`quickShortlist`) but is not used here. [`d-voices.png`]
4. **The pack is described by its ingredients, not by what it is.** "Voice pack. Apple Silicon: Parakeet and Whisper on MLX, Kokoro voices." (`VoicePackPanel.tsx:13-24`, `studio.json voice.lane.mlx`). What it actually contains (`backend_service/voice_env.py:87-96, 530-547`): the speech Python packages (about 1.9 GB), the Kokoro weights (about 330 MB), the turn detector (2 MB) and the speaker-label models (36 MB). The dictation models are *not* in it (they download from the STT cards, `VoiceModelsTab.tsx:84-90` via the generic `downloadModel`). So "voice pack" reads as "the models" when it is the runtime plus one small model, and the person cannot tell whether installing it gets them dictation (it does not, until a dictation model downloads on first use). [`d-models-no-pack.png`]
5. **Kokoro is three things on three screens.** On Models it is a lane description, a line "Kokoro: 49 voices in 8 languages" with Browse voices, and a subtitle on the speech-model panel ("The quick voices (Kokoro) come with the voice pack") (`VoiceModelsTab.tsx:133-166`, `TtsModelsPanel.tsx:118-120`). On Speak it is the "Quick" group with a note. On Voices it is the badge "Quick". Nowhere is it a model row like the other nine, so a person cannot compare it, see its size, or (after W6) understand why it is "the voice Chat falls back to" (`voice_chat.py` docstring). The round-two complaint ("Kokoro felt like the forced default") is fixed on Speak (`defaultVoice`, `speakLogic.ts:278-287`) but Chat still starts on `af_heart` (`voice_runtime.py:261`) and nothing on screen says the fallback rule.
6. **Settings are scattered across three places with no rule.** Dictation language and memory on Models ("Dictation and memory", `VoiceModelsTab.tsx:168-218`); chat voice, reading speed, auto-read, auto-send, call pause and interrupt at the bottom of Voices ("Chat and calls", `ChatCallsPanel.tsx`); agent cloning on Voices (`VoicesScreen.tsx:247-252`) *and* in Settings > Agent access ("Allow voice cloning", `AgentAccessPanel.tsx:150`). The Models panel even carries a subtitle pointing elsewhere ("How chat reads aloud and voice calls: Voice Studio > Voices"). A person changing how Chat reads must know to open Studio > Voices and scroll to the end. [`d-models-installed.png`, `d-voices.png`]
7. **Nine speech-model cards with a five-cell cost table each is the longest screen in the app, and the names do not say what to pick.** "Qwen3-TTS 0.6B Base", "1.7B Base", "0.6B CustomVoice", "1.7B CustomVoice", "1.7B VoiceDesign" read as five versions of one thing; the capability chip "SPEAK" appears on every card and means nothing; the 0.6B CustomVoice card does not say it is no faster than the 1.7B (FU-148, dropped, but the catalog note exists). Music solved this with the shared table shell: one row per model, a Fit badge, status, a Makes column and sort by fit (`src/components/models/ModelsPage.tsx`, `d-music-models.png`). Voice kept a custom page (`docs/plans/voice-rework/plan.md:44`). [`d-models-installed.png`]
8. **Chat gives no clue which voice speaks and points the wrong way.** The speaker button's title is "Read aloud"; the Call (i) lists "a voice for replies (Voice Models)" (`ChatTab.tsx:218`) and "Click Call to open Voice Models and install what is missing" (`:514`), but voices are chosen on Studio > Voices and, after W6, a reply may fall back to Kokoro with a note in the stream (`voice_chat.py`, `{"note", "voice"}` lines). The place to say "Ryan is reading; Heart answers calls; change" does not exist. [`d-chat.png`]

Then:

9. **Duplicate headings.** "Voice Studio" is both the page title (with "Record, transcribe, and speak") and the shell title (`VoiceStudioTab.tsx:135-136`); same for "Voice Library" (`VoiceLibraryTab.tsx:280`). The second heading costs a row at the top of every Studio screen. [`d-transcribe.png`]
10. **The cost chip "fast, under 1 GB" is repeated on every quick card** (six to twenty times per screen, `VoicePicker.tsx:74`), so the one chip that matters (a natural voice's memory and speed) does not stand out. [`d-speak-natural.png`]
11. **The cloning statement appears as a paragraph on Voices and again inside the Speak picker** (`VoicesScreen.tsx:141`, `VoicePicker.tsx:232`), before the person has shown any interest in cloning. It belongs in the New cloned voice form, where the permission tick is.
12. **"Browse voices" means two things.** On a cloning model's row it opens Voices (`TtsModelsPanel.tsx:74-76`); on the quick-voices line it also opens Voices (`VoiceModelsTab.tsx:157-161`); but on the Base model rows it reads as "see this model's voices", which a Base model has none of.
13. **"Downloads on first use if you skip this"** under every dictation card (`VoiceModelsTab.tsx:123-127`) tells the person that the radio button and the Download button are independent, which they will find out by trial.
14. **The Models page header and the sidebar caption use STT / TTS** (`tabs.ts:46`, `sidebarGroups.ts:18`); the rest of the app says dictation, read-aloud, speech. The unused key `tabShortLabels.voiceModels: "My Models"` is stale (`src/locales/en/common.json:107`).
15. **Install card wording ("The quick voices are fast but flat")** is right in spirit but is the only place that explains the difference between quick and natural (`InstallVoiceCard.tsx:43-47`); once dismissed ("Not now") the explanation is gone for the session.
16. **Transcribe is fine** (`d-transcribe.png`): the only gaps are that the Translate checkbox explains itself only in a tooltip and the drop zone does not list the file types.
17. **Library is fine**; "Start a storyboard with this" on every speech card is long, and there is no voice-kind filter when the list grows.

What is already good and must stay: Open Studio / Models / Library links both ways (W4); Speak's default-voice rule and the install card (W2); the graded shortlist and language select (W3); the measured cost cells (W1); autosave and Speak again (W7); the clone form with the permission tick; the mode memory.

## 3. Answers to the owner's five questions

**1. Delete voice models.** Today you cannot: no screen and no route deletes a speech or dictation model, the add-on or the pack (finding 1). Recommendation: add **Delete** to every model row through the same action menu and confirm dialog the other Models pages use, and a **Remove voice runtime** under Troubleshooting. Backend: dictation models are plain Hub repos fetched by the generic downloader, so `POST /api/models/download/delete` (`backend_service/state/downloads.py:572`) already works for them; speech models need one voice-specific route that removes the model's snapshot *and* its companion repos unless another installed row still uses them (`backend_service/catalog/voice_models.py` companions: the Chatterbox V3 default voice, the Orpheus loader and tokenizer, the S3 tokenizer), and leaves the 100 MB watermark component in place while any cloning model remains. The pack remove deletes `~/.chaosengine/voice-venv` and the marker and stops the worker first. Reason: disk is the one thing a person cannot get back today, and the pattern already exists elsewhere in the app, so the confirm text, the sibling list and the icons come free.

**2. Do Quick voices (Kokoro) need to be on the Voice Models page?** Not as their own panel. The voices themselves already left the page (W5). What remains is a one-line panel that only says a count and offers Browse voices and the Mandarin add-on, plus two more mentions in subtitles (finding 5). Recommendation: make Kokoro the first **row of the speech-model list** ("Kokoro: 22 quick voices in 2 languages. Fast on any machine; the voice Chat falls back to. Part of the voice runtime, 0.3 GB"), with Browse voices as its action, and move the Mandarin add-on to the runtime panel's add-ons. Delete the Quick voices panel and the two subtitle mentions. Reason: a newcomer compares models in one list; Kokoro is a model; after W6 its role (the fallback) is a fact about a model, not about the pack.

**3. Do Dictation and memory settings have to be on the Voice Models page?** They have to exist; they do not need their own panel. Recommendation: dictation language becomes a field in the header of the dictation-models panel (next to "Use for dictation", which is the other half of the same choice); "In memory now: Kokoro, 0.9 GB · Unload" and the idle minutes become one line in the runtime panel, because memory is a fact about the runtime. The "Dictation and memory" panel and its "chat lives elsewhere" subtitle go. The chat behaviour settings (auto-read, auto-send, speed, pause, interrupt) move to a **Voice popover in Chat** (open question 2 in plan.md), where they are used, and the Voices tab keeps only *which* voice does what. Reason: one rule a person can guess: a setting sits next to the thing it changes.

**4. Should the Voice Pack be split so users choose which models they get?** No split of the base; rename and explain it, and make the two add-ons explicit. What is inside (`voice_env.py`): about 1.9 GB of speech packages (needed for everything), Kokoro 330 MB (needed as the W6 fallback and the only voice that runs fast on CPU-only machines, D-72), the turn detector 2 MB (calls), speaker-label models 36 MB (transcripts), and nothing else: every dictation and speech model is already a separate choice on the same page. Splitting would save at most 370 MB and break calls, transcripts with speakers or the fallback for whoever unticks the wrong box. Recommendation: call it **Voice runtime**, list the four parts with sizes on the panel, show the add-ons (Mandarin quick voices; on Windows and Linux the speech GPU add-on) as tick boxes on the install card and the installed panel (the backend already takes `extras`, `voice_env.start_install`), and add Remove. Reason: the person's real question is "what am I downloading and why 2 GB"; a list answers it, a split does not.

**5. Voice Studio is unintuitive.** Agreed, for three reasons the screenshots make plain: Speak hides its form under the picker (finding 2); Voices is a flat list that mixes voice choice, cloning, agent policy and chat settings (findings 3, 6, 11); and the header repeats itself (finding 9). Recommendation: (a) Speak shows **one voice row** (name, kind, cost, sample, Change voice) and opens the existing picker in a **drawer**, so the first screen is text, voice, style, Generate; the per-model controls (tags, Expression, language, cloning model) sit under the voice row because they belong to it. (b) Voices gets a **main-voices strip** at the top (Speak, Chat read-aloud, Voice calls: the three W6 settings) and a list **grouped like the picker** (Natural, Quick shortlist with an expander, Cloned, Designed) with one "Use for…" menu per row; the cloning statement moves into the New cloned voice form; the agent switch moves to Settings > Agent access where it already has a twin; the chat behaviour toggles move to Chat. (c) The shell title row replaces the page title. Transcribe needs copy only. Mock-ups: sections 2, 3 and 6 of [mockups.html](mockups.html).

**The earlier complaint (Kokoro as the forced default).** Fixed on Speak by W2 (best installed natural voice first); fixed in Chat by W6 (any voice, a voice per use). What remains is to *show* it: the main-voices strip on Voices and the Voice popover in Chat name the voice in use and the fallback rule, and Kokoro's model row says it is the fallback.

## 5. What was not verified

- No audio was made or listened to; every screen ran against the mocked voice API in a throwaway HOME (the backend had no pack installed).
- W6's frontend does not exist yet; the proposal reads its backend (`callVoice`, `speak/stream` with `use`, the note line, the chat cost fields) and assumes its UI touches `VoicesScreen.tsx`, `ChatCallsPanel.tsx`, `speakLogic.ts` (`usableInChat`), `ReadAloud.tsx`, `useVoiceCall.ts`, `speechPlayer.ts`, `ChatTab.tsx`, `src/types/voice.ts`, `src/api/voice.ts`, the locales and `e2e/tests/14-voice-speak.spec.ts`. plan.md sequences after those.
- The mocks list two languages of quick voices (22) while the real pack lists 49 in 8; the Models page in `d-models-installed.png` shows the real count on the quick line and the mock count elsewhere.
- Windows and Linux were not looked at; the proposal assumes the portable lane shows Quick and Cloned only, as `modelsAvailable` already does.
- The Chat Call (i) popover was not captured: it only renders with a loaded chat model.
