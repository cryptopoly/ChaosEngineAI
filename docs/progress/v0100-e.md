# v0.10.0 package S-E: rough-cut preview and captions (S9, S13)

Branch `v0100/e`, from `release/v0.10.0`. Plan: `docs/plans/v0.10.0-storyboard-polish.md`, package S-E.

## Done

### S9 Rough-cut preview
- "Play film" on the Storyboard tab (beside Add shot), the Mixer (beside Render preview, now the primary button
  there) and the Export tab ("Before you export" header). It opens a dialog that plays the film from the files
  that already exist. Nothing is rendered: there was no need, the browser can sequence the files.
- How it plays (`src/features/storyboard/preview/`):
  - `roughCutLogic.ts` (pure, tested): the cut from the project timeline (the one the Mixer draws), what is on
    screen at a time (crossfades overlap and fade up), the source second each clip shows (trims and speed),
    when each sound starts, its fades, music ducked under voice (about -12 dB while a voice line plays, the
    export uses a compressor), captions on film time, keys, shot steps, scrubber markers.
  - `useRoughCut.ts`: fetches each file once (blob, the route needs the token), decodes every sound with Web
    Audio (audio clips, and each clip's own sound at the shot's gain and speed) and schedules them on the
    audio clock, sample-exact. The picture follows that clock: one muted video per shot, layered and faded,
    a hidden clip started 70 ms before its cut, a seek only when it is more than 100 ms off, a clip at its
    end holds its last frame. The picture is drawn late by the audio output latency. A 40 ms timer runs the
    same step as the animation frame, because frames drop to about 1 a second in a covered window.
  - `RoughCutPlayer.tsx` / `RoughCutButton.tsx`: stage in the export frame shape, shot chip, Draft badge,
    a still held with "No clip yet · still held for N s", a hatched card for a shot with no picture, the
    captions drawn like the burn-in (CC toggle, C key), scrubber over the whole film with numbered shot
    markers (clip, draft, held still, nothing) and a legend, loading progress, notes for missing shots,
    drafts and unreadable files. Keys: Space/K, J/L 5 s, arrows 1 s (Shift 10 s), Page Up/Down or , and .
    by shot, Home/End, C, Esc. Modal dialog with focus trap; slider with value text naming the shot.
- Shots with no clip yet keep their place in the rough cut (still or card for the planned length); the
  export leaves them out, so the dialog says so.
- Export tab fix found on screen: "Exported · size · length" showed the project timeline length (13 s) for a
  9.4 s film when shots had no clip. It now shows `exportedSeconds(timeline)` (ready shots, less crossfades).

### S13 Captions
- Most of S13 already existed (speech-to-text "Make from voice" in the Mixer, editing, `.srt` sidecar beside
  the film, burn-in switch, `storyboard_captions export_srt` tool). Extended, not duplicated:
- Burn-in look: `captions.look_for(project, text)` gives position (bottom, top, middle), size (small, medium,
  large) and colour from the project's caption look when one exists (`project.brand.captionLook` or
  `project.export.captionLook`, dict or object, fields `position`, `size`, `colour`/`color`, `font`), else
  white, bottom, medium. The font is a system font with the needed glyphs (Helvetica Neue / Hiragino Sans on
  macOS, Segoe UI / Microsoft YaHei on Windows, DejaVu Sans / Noto Sans CJK SC on Linux): the default Arial
  drew Japanese as empty boxes. `assemble_graphs.caption_force_style` turns the look into libass
  `force_style` (bold, outline, shadow; portrait frames get type scaled to the width). Every value is
  checked twice (render plan boundary and graph builder) so no free text reaches the filter.
- Found: `force_style` on an SRT takes the legacy SSA alignment numbers (top centre is 6, middle 10, not
  8 and 5); checked on frames.

## Seen on screen (Mac, own backend 8881, vite 8926, browser pane)
Throwaway project "S-E rough cut check (throwaway)": 3 clips copied from Tea Shop Ad 2 and 1 from Petal &
Stem Ad (import op), one take marked draft, a crossfade into shot 3, one shot with only a still, one with
nothing, the voice line, music (ducked, fade out) and an effect from Petal & Stem. Captions made with "Make
from voice" (3 cues). Desktop 1440x900 and phone 375x812.
- Player: loads 8 files, plays all 6 shots, chip and Draft badge change with the shot, crossfade opacity,
  still banner, card, captions on and off, Escape closes, keyboard seeks and shot steps land where expected.
- Sync measured, not by ear: every sound scheduled (4 shot sounds from AAC, voice WAV, music and effect
  FLAC). Voice: the preview starts it at film 0.500 s from source 0, so its first word at 0.802 s (the file
  has 0.302 s of lead silence, ffmpeg silencedetect); in the exported voice stem the first word is at
  0.802 s. Shot starts in the preview match the export's frame-exact joins (1.708, 2.917, 4.625 s).
  Picture against the sound clock in the browser pane, 3 full plays: median 22 to 28 ms, 95th percentile 32
  to 70 ms, picture ahead of the sound by about 30 ms on average (the tolerant direction); the first play
  after a reload made one seek for a 428 ms start-up stall. Measured with the player's own drift figures
  (data attributes on the dialog), while the pane's animation frames were throttled to 1.5 per second.
- Export with SRT + Burn in + stems from the Export tab: 9.4 s MP4, `.srt` beside it with the three cues,
  frames at 1.5 s and 4 s show the captions burnt in, white bold, bottom centre. The preview caption size
  was then matched to the burnt-in line width.

## NOT tested
- Sync in the desktop app's own webview (WKWebView); only the browser pane (Chromium). The pane's animation
  frames were throttled, so smoothness of crossfades at 60 fps was not seen.
- Real-device phone; only phone width in the pane.
- Long films (memory: every sound is decoded in full; about 23 MB per minute of stereo).
- A sped-up or slowed shot in the player: the shot's own sound is resampled (pitch changes), the export
  keeps pitch (atempo). Unit tested only.
- Portrait (9:16) burn-in frames, and the caption look from a real S15 field (none exists on this branch).
- Windows and Linux fonts for the burn-in.
- Lyric captions (optional in the brief): not built.
