## Done

### S11 Redo clip only, redo still only
- Batch kinds `redo-clip` and `redo-still` (`backend_service/storyboard/batch.py`; `BatchRecord.what` in `models.py`).
  `redo-clip`: a new take that keeps the active still and has a new clip. `redo-still`: a new take with a new still that
  keeps the old clip (and its length) until it is remade. Both ask the makers for a new seed (a copy of the shot with no
  seed; the shot's own seed and fingerprint stay), keep the pass of the take they redo (a draft is redone as a draft, at
  the draft size), refuse locked shots unless forced, and skip shots with nothing to redo (an upload; a still shot with no
  still yet; `redo-still` on a shot that does not start from a still). With no shot ids they work on every unlocked shot
  that has a take.
- The dependency rule: a clip now records the still it started from (`settings.clipStill`). `deps.clip_outdated` marks a
  shot stale when its take's still is not that one. Takes made before this have no `clipStill` and never turn stale by it.
  The next Make (or Redo clip) remakes only the clip, from the new still; a stills batch leaves such a shot alone.
- Takes also record `stillSeed` and `clipSeed` (the old single `seed` stays).
- Routes: `POST .../batches` and `POST .../estimate` take both kinds. The estimate prices them (catalogue or this
  project's measured takes) or returns `seconds: null` with the missing model named. A redo of draft takes is priced as a
  draft. Agent: `storyboard_generate` accepts both in its `what` enum, so the S1 precheck and the S7 plan card price them
  through the same `plan_steps` and `estimate`.
- UI: shot card menu "Redo clip only" and "Redo still only". A clip behind its still shows "New still · clip not remade
  yet", a short "Clip is behind" badge and a "Redo clip" button in place of Re-run. The shot drawer has "Redo clip only ·
  about 1 min 21 s" / "Redo still only · about 12 s" (or "· time unknown") above Make this shot, and Make this shot now
  shows its own estimate (it showed "unknown" before).

### S10 Take compare
- `TakeCompare.tsx` (new): two takes side by side (opens on the active take and the newest other one; each side has a
  take picker that cannot pick the other side's take). Clips or Stills switch when both kinds exist. Clips play in step:
  one Play both / Pause, one scrubber, the longer clip leads and both loop, sound from Left / Right / Off. Each side has a
  Draft or Final badge, In use, and "Use this one" (the `pick_take` op; the dialog stays open and announces the change).
  A table of what made each take (pass, still model and seed, clip model and seed, size, length) marks the rows that
  differ (dot and highlight, and "(differs)" for screen readers), with a one-line summary ("Same still, a different
  clip." and so on). Modal with focus trap and Escape; on a narrow screen the panes stack, it opens as a sheet and Play
  both sticks to the bottom.
- Opened from a "Compare" button beside the takes strip (two takes or more) and "Compare takes" in the shot card menu.
- Logic in `remakeLogic.ts` (pure, tested).

### S12 Sample project
- `backend_service/storyboard/sample/`: `film/` holds "The Lighthouse" (four shots of 2 s, 16:9 480p 24 fps, one take
  each, no batches or history, no model pinned so a redo uses whatever is installed), 1.4 MB in all. `install()` packs it as a
  bundle and runs the ordinary guarded `import_bundle`, so the copy is a normal project with a new id (edit, redo,
  export, delete), named in the user's language when the app sends a name.
- Routes (end of `routes/storyboard.py`): `GET /api/storyboard/sample` (card facts, `available`), `GET
  /api/storyboard/sample/poster` (first still), `POST /api/storyboard/sample` (`{name?}`, returns the project payload).
- UI: `SampleProjectCard.tsx` ("Try the sample project", poster, "4 shots · 7 s · 16:9 · 480p", "Open the sample", a
  busy label and an error callout) under the empty-state on an empty Projects list; hidden when the install has no
  sample. A "Sample film" template in New project (`projectTemplates.ts`); the dialog hides the format and model fields
  for it and says what it does (smallest hook in `ProjectDialog.tsx`).
- Release staging: `backend_service` is already copied whole; `scripts/stage-runtime.mjs` now names the folder
  (`BUNDLED_SAMPLE_FILM`) and fails the staging if any file the sample's `project.json` names is missing.
  `tests/test_storyboard_sample.py` checks that, and that no ignore rule of the staging would drop a sample file.
- `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`: a "Sample film (bundled media)" section.

How the media was made: on this Mac with the app itself (own backend on 8882), a project at 480p with four 2 s shots,
Automatic models (Iris-3B Turbo stills, about 11 to 13 s each; LTX-2.5 distilled q4 clips, about 67 to 75 s each), one
Make. The clips are exactly as the app wrote them (41 frames, 832x512, 1.7 s, 170 to 490 KB, metadata untouched). The
stills came out at 1360x768 PNG (Iris raises sizes to 768 px), about 1.2 MB each, so they were resized to 854x480 JPEG
(25 to 56 KB) and their hashes and `clipStill` updated, so the copy opens with every shot done and nothing stale.
Every file is under the repository's 500 KB large-file check.

Licence reasoning: Iris-3B Turbo is Apache 2.0 (catalogue row), no restriction on outputs. LTX-2.5 is under the LTX-2
community licence (read in the downloaded weights' `LICENSE`): section 5 says the licensor claims no rights in the
output and the user is accountable for it; section 6 asks that disclosures, metadata and provenance on outputs are not
removed, so the clips ship unchanged. The prompts are original. No weights ship. This is a reading of the text, not
legal advice.
