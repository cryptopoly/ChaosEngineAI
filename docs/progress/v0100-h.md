# v0.10.0 package S-H: the open gaps of the Storyboard work

Branch `v0100/h`, from `release/v0.10.0`. Picks up the follow-ups of S-D (`v0100-d.md`) and S-E (`v0100-e.md`).

## Done

### 1. Export draws the brand kit
- Export tab, new section "Brand on the film" (`BrandCardsPanel.tsx`): logo in the corner of every shot (on by
  default when the brand kit has a logo), title card at the start, end card at the end, their text (empty means the
  project name) and how long each lasts (1 to 5 s, default 2 s). New `ExportSettings` fields: `showLogo`, `titleCard`,
  `endCard`, `titleText`, `endText`, `cardSeconds` (defaults keep older projects as they were; no schema bump).
- The look (`storyboard/cards.py`, mirrored in `preview/brandCards.ts`): typeface from the brand caption font
  (`fonts.font_file`, else the platform default), background from the first style reference with a palette (else
  black), text colour from the brand caption colour when it reads on that background (contrast 3:1), else white or
  black. The card shows the logo above the text.
- Render plan: `title_card`, `end_card`, `logo` (`render_plan.CardInput`, `LogoInput`), checked at the boundary:
  card text one printable line of at most 200 characters, colours `#RRGGBB`, font file absolute with a font suffix,
  logo absolute with an image suffix, corner from the four.
- Assembler: a card is a cached intermediate like a shot (`card-<hash>.mov`), made from a `color` source, the logo
  overlay and `drawtext`. The text never reaches a filter: it is written to a work file that `drawtext` reads with
  `textfile=` and `expansion=none`; the font file is copied into the work folder under a fixed safe name. Cards join
  by a cut; sound placed by time and the captions move later by the title card (whole frames); anchored sound follows
  its shot. The corner logo is an `overlay` over the shots only (`enable=between(t,...)`, numbers only), drawn under
  the captions; with the logo the film is re-encoded. Only the pinned bundled ffmpeg runs. Without `drawtext` a card
  shows its colour and logo and the render says so.
- Captions burn-in now uses the brand caption look (`brand.captions`: font, colour, position; S-E read only
  `captionLook`), and libass gets the brand font's file through `fontsdir`.
- Preview renders (the Mixer's "Render preview") leave the cards out so the Mixer playhead stays on its timeline;
  the logo stays. The full export and "Play film" have the cards.
- Rough-cut player: the cards as their own layers (same colours, typeface, logo and text positions as the export),
  the corner logo over the shots only, captions in the brand look (colour, font, position), scrubber markers and a
  legend entry for the cards. The picture part moved into `preview/RoughCutStage.tsx` (shared with the Mixer).

### 2. Style changes mark shots stale
- `deps.fingerprint` adds the style for a take whose `settings.style` is `pictures` (the first picture of each style in
  use) or `words` (each style's words and palette), plus `brand.styleUse`. Changing a style's pictures, words or
  palette, adding or removing a style, or the brand kit's style switch, marks only the shots that used the style as
  stale. Nothing is regenerated; Undo brings them back. The logo and caption look do not mark shots: they are drawn on
  the export, not into a shot. Takes made before (no `settings.style`) keep their fingerprint.

### 3. Agent tools
- `storyboard_project` gains `plan` (brief, film_seconds; 0 clears), `brand` (logo as an upload handle or output file,
  "" removes; logo_corner; style_use; caption {font, colour, position}, the font must be installed), `kind: style` on
  add_ref and `palette` on edit_ref. Same revision check and scope (`generate`) as the other edits; style references
  show in the summary's `cast`. Golden snapshot regenerated with `UPDATE_MCP_GOLDEN=1 pytest tests/test_mcp_contract.py`
  after the change (the diff is only these fields and the shorter description).

### 4. Mixer transport plays the rough cut
- The Mixer's Play / Stop / Loop drive the rough cut from the takes (`mixer/useMixerRoughCut.ts`), no render needed;
  the stage shows the rough cut, the playhead follows its clock, seeking on the timeline moves it, Loop starts over,
  and an edit while playing carries on with the new cut. The Mixer's cut has no cards, so its clock is the timeline it
  draws. "Render preview" is unchanged; once a preview exists a switch ("Rough cut" / "Rendered preview") picks what
  Play plays.
- `useRoughCut` now fetches only files it does not have yet (a Mixer edit does not refetch the film) and takes an
  `onEnded` hook.
