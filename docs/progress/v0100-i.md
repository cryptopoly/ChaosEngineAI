## Chat plan card (integrator's item, S-G's report)

Not reproduced. With Qwen3.8-27B MLX 4-bit and Studio tools on (own backend and storyboards folder), five runs: three
through the API stream, two through the Chat screen (thinking off and auto, one asking for a long treatment before the
call). Every time the card appeared once, approving it ran the batch and the model's answer arrived. What was seen:
- The model's first call is often refused by the S1 pre-check (`what: "stills"`, a redo on shots with no take). That
  shows as a tool card with an error, not a plan card, and the model retries. If the model gives up after the error,
  the user sees "the model called storyboard_generate, no card, no batch". Defect 6 made one such refusal misleading.
- Defect 8 is the likely cause of "the reply came back empty": any tool-calling reply that takes more than 120 s on MLX
  ends with the worker killed. Reproduced through the Plan tab, not through Chat in these runs.
- Leaving a running Chat thread (or reloading) while its card waits: the thread list shows the running thread as "New
  chat" until it finishes, so it is easy to miss; after a reload the card is gone and the turn waits up to 30 minutes.
  By design (S1 docs), worth a note in the UI.
