## What changed

| # | Change | Files |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Typed text whose save is refused is kept per field. A small store (`unsavedText.ts`) lives with the open project in `useStoryboard` (`story.unsaved`, given to the tabs by `ProjectShell` as a context); `useStoryboard`'s `mutate` records why a change failed (`conflict` or `failed`). One hook, `useUnsavedText` (`useUnsavedText.tsx`), is used by every blur-save field: it sends, follows the answer, and renders "Your text was not saved" with the user's text and **Put it back** (after "Changed elsewhere; showing the latest" adopted the latest) or **Retry** (the backend did not answer), plus "Keep the latest" / "Discard my text". Put it back / Retry restore the text into the field and save it again. A field that unmounts with unsent typing (the tab shows "Waiting for the backend…") leaves its text in the store and shows it when it is back. Not disabled while busy (the op queue orders it). Fields (21): brief; Plan shot row title, seconds, picture and movement prompts; shot card title; title and end card text; cast name and description; project name; lyrics; section labels (notice under the ruler); caption text; loudness; the six mixer number fields (fades, trims, anchor offset, start). Callbacks that used `void story.op(...)` now return the op's promise. | `unsavedText.ts`, `useUnsavedText.tsx`, `unsaved.css` (new); `useStoryboard.ts`, `ProjectShell.tsx`, `PlanBrief.tsx`, `PlanShotRow.tsx`, `ShotCard.tsx`, `StoryboardTab.tsx`, `ProjectShellHeader.tsx`, `BrandCardsPanel.tsx`, `ExportTab.tsx`, `ReferenceCard.tsx`, `CastTab.tsx`, `song/SongLyrics.tsx`, `song/SongRuler.tsx`, `song/SongPanel.tsx`, `mixer/CaptionsPanel.tsx`, `mixer/MixerTab.tsx`, `mixer/ClipInspector.tsx`, `mixer/ExportPanel.tsx` |

Rows are named "Shot 3 · Title" and have a focus ring. 19 shots: 6 stops through the list instead of about 95. | `PlanShotList.tsx` (new), `PlanShotRow.tsx`, `PlanTab.tsx`, `plan.css` |
