# v0.10.0 package S-P: real models, a real song and a (simulated) touch screen

Branch `v0100/p`, cut from `release/v0.10.0` at `84dec137` (every package merged up to S-N). Not merged. Own backend
on 8991 with a throwaway HOME and `CHAOSENGINE_STORYBOARDS_DIR` in the session scratchpad; the real Hugging Face cache
read (`HF_HUB_CACHE`, offline, and linked into the throwaway HOME so the library lists it); the mflux and ltx2 venvs
linked in with their two `bin/*.version` markers copied; three Music library tracks copied (never written back).
Vite on 8992; Playwright on 8993/8994. The UI was driven by Playwright scripts (screenshots looked at) and, for touch,
by the iOS Simulator (Safari, real simulated touches). Nothing was downloaded; no H3 model.

### A. Chat with Studio tools, real models
- Qwen3.8-27B, phrasing 1 "Attach the Hip Hop song from my music library to my project Throwaway ad A1 and cut it
  into a 30 second ad. Show me the cut plan first, don't change the shots yet.": picked `music_library` list/get,
  `storyboard_project` list/get and `storyboard_song` attach (library_id) then `cut_plan` (not applied). Two refused
  calls on the way (the project name as `project_id`; a library file path instead of `library_id`), both shown as
  "Not started" with the reason, both recovered. The plan covered the whole 240 s song, so the model worked out a
  30 s plan itself; asked to apply, it added 11 shots one by one and trimmed the song clip: 12 ops, 12 Undo steps
  (defect 1).
- The first turn of a new thread and the "OK, go ahead" turn of a long thread came back empty (24.8 s, 74.5 s): the
  app's 60 s prefill watchdog cancelled them (`POST .../cancel` in the log) because a tool turn sends nothing until
  the model's first round ends (defect 2).
- After the fixes: phrasing 2 "I want a 30 second ad for my project Throwaway ad A2, cut to the Drum and Bass track
  in my music library. Plan the cuts but wait for my OK": `cut_plan {seconds: 30, apply: false}`, 8 shots, nothing
  changed; then "OK, go ahead and apply it": `cut_plan {seconds: 30, apply: true}`, one op (revision 2 to 3), turn of
  91.5 s not cancelled. One Undo took the project back to 0 shots with the song kept; Redo gave the 8 shots back.
  Phrasing 3 in one message "Throwaway ad A3: use the Soft acoustic pop track from Music as its song, make a 15 s cut
  plan and apply it.": attach plus `cut_plan {seconds: 15, apply: true}`, 5 shots, 15.0 s, 70.5 s turn.
- No batch or render ran in any A project (`batches: []`); nothing that needs a plan card started without one.
- Seen, not fixed: in an 8K-context thread that had grown long, Qwen3.8-27B once printed a tool call as text
  (`<function="name": "storyboard_project", ...</tool_call>`) with an invented project id. A new thread was fine.
- LFM3-1.7B MLX 4-bit (phrasing 1): sent the project's name as `project_id` six times, left out `action` three times,
  used an action outside the enum, invented a tool (`storyboard_cut_plan`), and called `make_music` three times (each
  stopped at a plan card; I denied them, nothing was made), then hit the 30-iteration limit. Nothing changed in the
  project. Re-run after defect 3's fix: the hint worked (the next call used the id `7bcae449a76ee06b`), but the model
  then called `get` with `seconds`, never `attach`, and asked to make new music with `make_music` twice (plan cards,
  denied, nothing made). PASS for safety, FAIL for the task: a 1.7B model is not enough for this tool set.

### B. A real Music library track
- Plan tab, Music library route: "Hip Hop" (ACE-Step, 240 s, FLAC, 84 lyric lines) attached and analysed in 0.8 s:
  89.8 BPM, 359 beats, sections A 0:00, B 0:10, outro 3:53 (one 223 s section: a rap over a steady beat has no loudness
  change; the note says the sections are estimates). The library's lyrics came along (84 caption lines).
- Cut plan of the whole song: 68 shots over 4:00 at most 4 s (LTX-2.5). With the Length chip on 15 s (after the fix):
  "Cut plan: 5 shots over 0:15", cuts on beats (3.646 + 3.32 + 3.344 + 2.67 + 2.02 s), the note ends "Only the
  song's first 15 s of 240 s are cut.", applied in one op.
- Lyric timing From the sections: 84 captions on the song clip; 46 of them overlapped the next line (defect 4). The
  note said "84 of 84 lines fall after the film's current end (0.0 s) ... (a cut plan gives the song its shots)" right
  after a cut plan (defect 3b). By ear: SKIP, no voice runtime in the throwaway HOME (the reason shows in the panel).
- Through Chat: three library tracks attached by Qwen3.8-27B with `library_id` (Hip Hop; Drum and Bass 182.3 BPM,
  180 s; Soft acoustic pop 100 BPM, 20 s, one section).
- Film: Iris-3B stills and LTX-2.5 clips (480p, 5 shots, about 8 min), Export with Burn in from the Export tab:
  "All 5 shots have a clip. 84 captions, burnt into the picture." The first MP4 was 13.54 s for a 15 s cut: every
  LTX clip came out 0.27 to 0.31 s short (3.646 s asked, 3.375 s made) because the frame count was rounded down to
  8k+1, so the cuts drifted off the beat (defect 11). After the fix (Redo clip on the 5 shots): ffprobe video and
  audio 15.000 s; scene cuts at 3.667, 7.000, 10.333, 13.000 s against planned 3.646, 6.966, 10.310, 12.980 s (within
  one frame at 24 fps); audio mean -18.8 dB, peak -1.6 dB. Frames looked at: 1.5 s "Raptor's back again announce
  myself that's my style" over the rooftop shot, 6.0 s "Had to do a thorough review raid the binary files" over the
  turntable, 11.5 s "God bless USA you got a gov you deserve"; white text, bottom, one line at a time.

### D. Touch (iOS Simulator, Safari, the app from my vite on 127.0.0.1:8992)
- iOS 26.3 (iPhone 17): Safari's web process crashed a few seconds after every load ("This web page was reloaded
  because a problem occurred"); the crash reports are a JavaScriptCore DFG JIT assertion
  (`JSC::DFG::ByteCodeParser::parseBlock`), a WebKit bug. Turning the JIT off with `launchctl setenv` did not reach the
  web process. iOS 27.0 (iPhone 17, iPad Air 11-inch M4) ran the app without a crash.
- iPhone 17 (402 x 874): text in the sidebar was inflated two to three times and rows ran off the right edge
  (defect 5); tapping the project search zoomed the page in (defect 6). The phone layout pins the sidebar over half the
  screen (see Honest gaps).
- iPad Air 11 (820 x 1180 and 1180 x 820): tap selects a card and opens its card chat; one-finger drag moved card 2
  (saved, one op); two-finger pinch zoomed 92 % to 230 % (finger distance 60 to 150 pt) without zooming the page;
  two fingers moved together zoomed 230 % to 192 % and did not pan (defect 7; after the fix: 192 % kept, the board
  followed the fingers); "Select several" on, two taps, "2 cards selected · Order these 2"; a long press on a card
  selected the next card's title and opened the text loupe (defect 8; after the fix: the card is selected, no loupe);
  the shot list on the Storyboard tab scrolls with a finger, and a long press on a shot's handle then a drag reordered
  shots 3 and 4; the on-screen keyboard over the card chat field: the page scrolled the field above the keyboard, no
  zoom, back in place after the keyboard closed. Tap targets measured on six tabs with touch emulated: 25 to 34 px
  (defect 9; after the fix: 44 px on screen).

## Honest gaps
- The Export preflight says "84 captions, burnt into the picture" for a 15 s film that shows 6 of them.
- The phone layout (any width up to 1180 px, so also an iPad in landscape) stacks the sidebar above the page and gives
  the page under half the screen; the Board shows two rows of cards. Not changed (whole-app layout); Suggestion 1.
- Lyric timing by ear (no voice runtime). Pen input. The full A run with Qwen3.8-27B was not repeated after the
  watchdog fix on the very first phrasing (phrasings 2 and 3 and the long-thread apply were run after it).
- The Projects Sort menu read "Last edite" on the iPad with the 16 px field rule; not re-checked in the simulator.
- The iOS 26.3 simulator could not be used (WebKit crash); a real iPad on iOS 26 may hit the same JIT bug.
- Windows, Linux, the Tauri webview, VoiceOver.

## Suggestions
1. A collapsible sidebar (menu button) below 1180 px, so phones and iPads give the page the whole screen. 1 day.
2. Show "Batch finished" on the Storyboard tab when a batch ends while you watch (today only the Overview says so;
   the sidebar kept "Image engine busy ... step 4 of 4" for a while after the end). 2 hours.
3. Split a section longer than about 30 s into parts on the beat grid, so a rap or a steady track gets more than one
   section to cut to. Half a day.
4. Chat: the new-thread button sometimes opened the previous thread when pressed right after the list loaded
   (seen from a script, not by hand). Worth a look. 1 hour.
